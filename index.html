The late-night talks. The goodbyes. The rooms that become yours.

Exora is written by residents, nomads and hosts, and printed as an A5 magazine, so you can still pack it with you.

Places and people change, the stories remain.
Exora explores the human side of living together on the move. No property valuations, no funding rounds, no polished brochures. Those have their own media.

Instead you'll find honest storytelling from residents and hard-won advice from hosts, so that nomads, residents and hosts can build real belonging wherever they land.

You don't need to be a writer to have a story. 
You don't even need to spell it out, we welcome art pieces and photography too.

“A bunch of hippies.” “A way to rip off young professionals.”

We hear everything and anything about coliving.

The overuse of “coliving” as an umbrella term for anything shared-housing can sometimes create a bad rep, or plain misunderstanding, around destination coliving. It would be sad to let a few bad apples make everyone dismiss the whole idea. Exora is where the people who actually live it make their voices heard and take back the narrative.

Readers & storytellers

Like in every memorable coliving, no one is just a guest. Everyone co-creates the experience.

Exora works the same way. The people who read it are the people who write it, photograph it, draw it and decide what goes in. You're not just a subscriber.

What we publish

These are the sections we can think of now. They might evolve depending on contributions. 

  • Behind the Front Door

    Hosts on house meetings, boundaries, conflict, and the weight of holding space for dozens of transient lives.

  • Life on the Road

    Goodbyes, nomad fatigue, and how to build deep friendships fast without losing the old ones.

  • Rituals and Making a Home

    The objects, habits and Sunday dinners that turn a rented bedroom into a sanctuary.

  • Slow Destinations and Local Roots

    Places seen by staying long enough to belong, not through tourist lists.

  • Visual Essays

    Documentary-style photography: messy kitchens, shared desks, communal meals, annotated floor plans.

  • Send a Toucan

    The last word goes to gratitude: a thank-you, a big up or a shout-out to a housemate, a host or a space that made shared living special. You too can make someone's day.

How to pitch

Email a 2 to 3 sentence summary of your idea, or your full draft as a Google Doc or PDF.

Add a short bio, such as "digital nomad currently in Lisbon" or "co-founder of a 10-person coliving space in rural Spain".

For photos, share a link to a folder of low-res previews.

For cover art, send a rough sketch or idea of how you'd picture life in a shared home.

In return

We can offer you a printed copy, promotion across our network, and sincere gratitude. We promise that if we ever make any money, you'll be the first to know.
