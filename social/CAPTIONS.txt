1 · WEDNESDAY 11 NOVEMBER · Myth vs reality
Image: 1_Wed-11-Nov_myth-vs-reality.png

"Coliving is just a hostel for grown-ups."
We hear it all the time. And sure, there's a shared fridge and someone always finishes the oat milk.

But ask anyone who's lived it and you'll hear about the friend who became family in three weeks, the host who held a house together through a winter, the dinner that changed a plan.

So: is it just a hostel for grown-ups? Tell us in the comments. The best answers might make it into Issue 01. ✏️

3 · MONDAY 16 NOVEMBER · Our story
Image: 3_Mon-16-Nov_born-at-a-dinner-table.png

Every good idea in a shared house starts at the dinner table. This one did too.

October 2026, a coliving space in Devon. Somewhere between the second helping and the washing up, a friend asked: "Why not make a magazine about this life?" And then: "I believe in you."

There was no going back after that.

Exora is for everyone who's ever turned a house full of strangers into a home. Issue 01 lands in Winter 2027, and you can be in it.

6 · MONDAY 23 NOVEMBER · Meet the editor

Hi, I'm Morgane 👋

Writer, coliver, and host at Selgars Mill in Devon, where my days start with coffee and end at a shared dining table.

I started Exora because the people who actually live in shared homes rarely get to tell their own stories. But Exora isn't my magazine. It belongs to everyone who has shared a home with strangers and ended up with friends. I'm just keeping the kettle on while you tell the stories.
